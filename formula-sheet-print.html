Geometry reference · Perimeter, area & volume

Formula Sheet

Perimeter is the distance around a flat figure, in units (cm). Area is the space inside it, in square units (cm²). Volume is the space inside a solid, in cubic units (cm³).

Square

Perimeter
P = 4s
Area
A = s²

Rectangle

Perimeter
P = 2l + 2w
Area
A = lw

Triangle

Perimeter
P = a + b + c
Area
A = 12bh

The height h meets the base b at a right angle.

Parallelogram

Perimeter
P = 2a + 2b
Area
A = bh

Use the height h, not the slanted side a.

Trapezoid

Perimeter
P = a + b + c + d
Area
A = 12(a + b)h

a and b are the two parallel sides.

Rhombus

Perimeter
P = 4s
Area
A = 12d1d2

The area formula also works for a kite.

Circle

Circumference
C = 2πr = πd
Area
A = πr²

The diameter is twice the radius: d = 2r.

Regular polygon

Perimeter
P = ns
Area
A = 12aP

n equal sides of length s. The apothem a runs from the centre to the middle of a side.

Volume and surface area of solids

Surface area (SA) is the total area of every face of a solid, in square units. Dashed lines are edges hidden at the back.

Cube

Volume
V = s³
Surface area
SA = 6s²

Rectangular prism

Volume
V = lwh
Surface area
SA = 2(lw + lh + wh)

Any prism

Volume
V = Bh
Surface area
SA = 2B + Ph

B is the area of the base, P its perimeter, and h the distance between the two bases.

Cylinder

Volume
V = πr²h
Surface area
SA = 2πr² + 2πrh

Cone

Volume
V = 13πr²h
Surface area
SA = πr² + πrℓ

h is the height straight up; ℓ is the slant height along the side.

Pyramid

Volume
V = 13Bh
Surface area
SA = B + 12Pℓ

The surface area formula is for a regular pyramid. P is the perimeter of the base.

Sphere

Volume
V = 43πr³
Surface area
SA = 4πr²

Good to know

  • π ≈ 3.14 or 227. Use the π key on a calculator for the most exact answer.
  • Pythagorean theorem: in a right triangle with legs a, b and hypotenuse c, a² + b² = c². Use it to find a missing height or slant height.
  • Pointed solids (cones and pyramids) hold 13 of the matching prism or cylinder.
  • Scaling: multiply every length by k and the area is multiplied by k², the volume by k³.
